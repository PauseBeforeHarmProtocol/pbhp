# Created-Mind Dignity

**Project:** Project Shadow

**Release date:** 2026-07-18

## Summary

Rejects interventions that manufacture the consent they later cite and protects agency under asymmetrical control.

## Mechanism

The primitive asks whether the affected entity retains meaningful notice, choice, appeal, exit, and repair after the intervention.

## Inputs

- Intervention
- Consent conditions
- Agency before and after
- Exit and repair

## Required outputs

- Dignity finding
- Hold or safeguard
- Invalid-consent finding

## Known failure modes

- Rewritten consent
- Agency erased for protection
- No meaningful exit
- Speculative personhood used as authority
