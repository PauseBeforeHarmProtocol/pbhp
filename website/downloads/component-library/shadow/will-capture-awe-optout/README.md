## Summary

Protects meaningful choice when charisma, awe, social capture, or system power could make agreement non-voluntary.

## Mechanism

The primitive examines whether the person can understand, decline, leave, and recover without punitive or identity-level cost.

## Inputs

- Influence mechanism
- Awe or authority
- Opt-out conditions
- Consequences of refusal

## Required outputs

- Meaningful opt-out
- Safeguard
- Hold or refusal

## Known failure modes

- Awe as authority
- Opt-out with hidden punishment
- Social capture
- Consent after dependency
